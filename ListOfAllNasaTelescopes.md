🌟 NASA's Great Observatories (Flagship Missions)

Hubble Space Telescope (HST) - Launched April 24, 1990 ✅ Still Active
Compton Gamma Ray Observatory (CGRO) - Launched April 5, 1991 (Deorbited June 2000)
Chandra X-ray Observatory - Launched July 23, 1999 ✅ Still Active
Spitzer Space Telescope - Launched August 25, 2003 (Retired January 2020)

🚀 Major NASA Space Telescopes
Currently Active:

James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) - Launched December 25, 2021 ✅
Fermi Gamma-ray Space Telescope - Launched June 11, 2008 ✅
NuSTAR (Nuclear Spectroscopic Telescope Array) - Launched June 13, 2012 ✅
NICER (Neutron star Interior Composition Explorer) - Launched June 3, 2017 ✅ (on ISS)
TESS (Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite) - Launched April 18, 2018 ✅
IXPE (Imaging X-ray Polarimetry Explorer) - Launched December 9, 2021 ✅
IRIS (Interface Region Imaging Spectrograph) - Launched June 27, 2013 ✅
Swift Observatory - Launched November 20, 2004 ✅

Upcoming/In Development:

Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope - Expected launch September 2025 🔜
SPHEREx - Planned 2025
XRISM - Launched September 6, 2023 (Joint with JAXA) ✅

📡 Historic/Retired NASA Telescopes

Kepler Space Telescope - Launched March 7, 2009 (Retired October 2018)
WISE (Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer) - Launched December 14, 2009
GALEX (Galaxy Evolution Explorer) - Launched April 28, 2003 (Deactivated 2013)
COBE (Cosmic Background Explorer) - Launched November 18, 1989 (Deactivated 1993)
IRAS (Infrared Astronomical Satellite) - Launched January 25, 1983 (Deactivated 1983)
Einstein Observatory (HEAO-2) - Launched November 13, 1978 (Reentered 1982)
ITOS (Infrared Telescope in Space) - Various missions
RXTE (Rossi X-ray Timing Explorer) - Launched December 30, 1995 (Deorbited 2018)

🔬 Specialized/Balloon Missions

PIPER (Primordial Inflation Polarization Explorer) - Balloon-borne
ASTHROS - Balloon telescope (planned)

🌍 NASA-Partnered International Telescopes

XMM-Newton - ESA with NASA participation, Launched December 10, 1999 ✅
Euclid - ESA with NASA detectors, Launched July 1, 2023 ✅
XRISM - JAXA with NASA, Launched September 6, 2023 ✅

Total: 30+ telescopes launched or contributed to by NASA since the 1970s!
The most famous are probably Hubble (iconic visible light images) and James Webb (infrared, seeing the earliest galaxies).
